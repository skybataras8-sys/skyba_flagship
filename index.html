Where Ukraine meets Brooklyn

Anna Skyba grew up in Prilimanske, right next to Odesa learning the art of pastry from her grandmother—techniques passed down through generations, recipes written in flour dust and memory. In 2019, she brought that knowledge to Brooklyn.

Today, with her partner Taras, she crafts each dessert by hand. No shortcuts. No industrial processes. Every torte, every petit four, every macaron is made fresh because that's how it should taste.

"Про любов у кожній деталі" — In every detail, love.

Skyba Dessert Studio is not a bakery. It's an atelier. A place where craftsmanship is not negotiable and time is never rushed.

How we create

Three things guide every creation: Ukrainian heritage, European technique, and an obsession with quality.

Ingredients

We source from Europe's finest producers. Belgian chocolate. French butter. Italian flour. Because what goes inside matters as much as how it looks.

Technique

Every dessert is made from scratch. Pâte à choux. Génoise. Mousse. Not recipes. Disciplines. Learned over years, practiced with intention.

Time

You cannot rush a good dessert. Some cakes rest overnight. Some flavors need three days. We wait for perfect because that's what you deserve.
